<h1 align="center">SlotWatch</h1>

<p align="center"><b>A personal assistant that lands the US‑visa appointment you want, before thousands of others take it.</b></p>

US‑visa appointment slots on the scheduling portal appear at random and vanish in seconds. Refreshing the site by hand for weeks, at 2 am, hoping to be the one who clicks first, is miserable, and you usually lose anyway.

SlotWatch watches the calendars for you, around the clock. The instant a slot opens inside the dates you chose, it books it, and tells you the moment it succeeds.

## What it does

- **Watches every city you care about**, in the order you care about them, and reads the calendar the way a careful person would.
- **Books inside your window only.** You set the earliest and latest date you would accept. A later date is never booked; you only get a notification.
- **Books the whole household** in one go when the portal lists your family together.
- **Keeps trying while the slot is still there.** A busy portal or a lost race is not the end of the calendar; SlotWatch moves to the next time and the next day before it gives up on a city.
- **Clears the usual hurdles by itself**: sign‑in, security questions, an expired session. You are told when, and only when, a human is really needed.
- **Runs on your schedule.** A steady rhythm, bursts around the minutes when new slots tend to drop, or both. A live preview shows the planned hour before you save it.
- **Shows you everything** in a local web dashboard: what it is doing right now, what it saw, what it booked, and a live view of the browser it drives.
- **Tells you on your phone** through your notification channel: slot found, booked, or attention needed.

## How you use it

1. Install it on the Windows or macOS machine where you sign in to the portal.
2. Open the dashboard, add the people, cities and dates, connect the browser.
3. Press **Start hunting** and walk away.

## Good citizen by design

SlotWatch automates **your own** account, for your own household, from your own machine. It paces itself to the portal's limits, never floods it, and never reads or books for anyone else. It is a personal tool, not a service, and it does not resell appointments.
