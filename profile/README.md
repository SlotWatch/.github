<h1 align="center">SlotWatch</h1>

<p align="center"><b>A personal assistant that lands the US‑visa appointment you want, before thousands of others take it.</b></p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://slotwatch.org">slotwatch.org</a></p>

US‑visa appointment slots on the scheduling portal appear at random and vanish in seconds. Refreshing the site by hand for weeks, at 2 am, hoping to be the one who clicks first, is miserable, and you usually lose anyway.

SlotWatch watches the calendars for you, around the clock. The instant a slot opens inside the dates you chose, it books it, and tells you the moment it succeeds.

## What it does

- **Watches the cities you choose**, in your order of preference. It reads each calendar like a careful person.
- **Books inside your window only.** You set the earliest and the latest date you accept. SlotWatch never books a slot outside that window. It sends you a notification instead.
- **Books for your household.** It books up to two people: the OFC biometrics appointment first, then the consular interview. It never reports a partial booking as done.
- **Keeps trying on the same calendar.** A day with no bookable time does not end the hunt. SlotWatch reads the next open day.
- **Clears the usual hurdles by itself**: the Cloudflare check, sign‑in, security questions and a sign‑out. It tells you only when it needs a person.
- **Runs on your schedule.** Pick a steady rhythm, bursts around the minutes when slots tend to drop, or both. A live preview shows the planned hour before you save it.
- **Shows you everything** in a local web dashboard: what it does now, what it saw, what it booked and a live view of its browser.
- **Alerts you on your phone** through the channel you choose: slot found, slot booked or attention needed.

## How you use it

One plan covers every device.

- **Desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux** run the hunt on the computer where you sign in to the portal.
- **iOS and Android apps** show the live view, the feed and the alerts. They start or stop the hunt. They never run it.
- **A managed cloud version** runs the hunt for you.

**Today:** the Windows `.exe` and the macOS `.dmg`, and Linux from source. **Planned:** the Linux app, the phone apps and the managed cloud version.

1. Install the desktop app on the computer where you sign in to the portal.
2. Open the dashboard. Add the people, the cities and the dates, then connect the browser.
3. Press **Start hunting** and walk away.

## Good citizen by design

SlotWatch automates **your own** account, for your own household, from your own computer. It paces itself to the portal's limits and never floods it. It never reads or books for anyone else. It is a personal tool, and it never resells appointments.

**Your data stays on your computer.** SlotWatch records all its traffic with the portal: requests, answers, cookies and page copies. It keeps these records in plain text in its data directory. The records never leave your computer, and SlotWatch receives none of them. The support bundle contains these records, so share it with care.
